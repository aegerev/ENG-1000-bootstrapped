# ENG-1000: Building Your English Foundation
Welcome to ENG-1000, your one-stop shop for building a solid foundation in English!

## Why a Foundation Matters:
Just like a house needs a strong base to stand tall, your English learning journey also needs a solid foundation. We believe traditional programs can leave gaps in your understanding. Here, we focus on building that essential foundation to ensure your success.

## What We Offer:
* Personalized Support: We're invested in your success and are here to guide you every step of the way.
* Engaging Learning: We explore topics that interest you, making learning enjoyable and stress-free.
* Practical Application: We connect English to real-world situations, making it relevant and useful.
* Lingolia Integration: Gain an "extra layer" of foundation through stories, conversations, and targeted exercises.

## Our Series:
* English Comp I (A0-A2): This introductory course is perfect for complete beginners or those needing a refresher. Learn important English fundamentals in a relaxed setting.
* English BootCamp (B1+): Take your English to the next level! This course delves deeper into real-life topics, pushing your understanding and fluency.
